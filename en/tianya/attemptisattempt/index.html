Any Attempt to Topple the Cultural Revolution Will Forever Remain Nothing but an Attempt -- It Can Never Be Realized.

Any rational person should know: any attempt to topple the Cultural Revolution will forever remain nothing but an attempt -- it can never be realized. The reason is simple: the Cultural Revolution is already a historical event, and history cannot be toppled. Anyone who attempts to topple history can only be toppled by history. Before the wheels of history, humans are less than a mantis.

If the rightists who attempt to topple the Cultural Revolution have water in their brains, then the leftists who attempt to prop up the Cultural Revolution to achieve some present-day objective don't even have brains at all. History cannot be toppled, nor can it be propped up, because the Cultural Revolution is long since history. Anyone entangled with the history of the Cultural Revolution is merely looking for excuses for various present-day schemes. The Cultural Revolution is just a giant garbage can -- both leftists and rightists can find their respective garbage in it.

The Cultural Revolution is long past. The Cultural Revolution was long ago ground to dust by history. The leftists' attempt to weave a pipe dream from the fragments of the Cultural Revolution will forever remain just a dream. Of course, everyone has the right to dream, and we need not stop the leftists from dreaming with all their might. As for those so-called Cultural Revolution victims, flying the rightist flag -- they, like the leftists, are equally trapped in the Cultural Revolution, locked in at a loss. Leftists and rightists are nothing but jackals of the same lair.

The Cultural Revolution is merely a hotbed for some people's nostalgia. Those who try to use the Cultural Revolution to lecture younger generations and prove their own rich life experience are even more laughable. Think about it -- your bunch got the country into such a sorry state, and you still have the nerve to show it off? If the Cultural Revolution is guilty, then anyone who survived during the Cultural Revolution is guilty, regardless of whether they're leftist or rightist. Want to play innocent? No way!

Leftists and rightists, if you lived during the Cultural Revolution era, then repent for your own crimes first! Stop making a ruckus on forums, using the Cultural Revolution as ammunition for your various purposes!
