Weekend Concert 28: Fantasy

2007/2/11 12:55:45

Having fewer fantasies is probably one of the greatest signs of aging. But fantasy is ultimately indispensable — modern society annihilates all Fantastique, everything crushed to dust under science and material desire. Even so, it all still rests upon a godlike Fantastique of science and material desire.

Killing fantasy is still fantasy -- merely the most tedious kind。

Mendelssohn "A Midsummer Night's Dream" Overture

A seventeen-year-old genius who wrote his most brilliant work right from the start --
this is perhaps also a kind of sorrow.

Chopin "Fantaisie-Impromptu"

Fantasy can also come from the earth.

Schumann "Träumerei"

When fantasy has warmth, it has light.

Berlioz "FantastiqueSymphony"

Love, hate, passion, vengeance -- blood and death can actually be fantasized like this
