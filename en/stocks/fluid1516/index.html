That Night, His Bodily Fluids Sprayed All Over Me (XV, XVI)

2006/11/28 12:05:08

XV


Blood, clearly crisscrossing the steam-wreathed mirror. Clamorous wind, silently engulfed. Roses, time's private parts, splitting open in blood-red. The Kunlun Mountains, dripping with blood; the Pacific, blood waves surging. Lightning, slashing across the blood-colored sky; meteors, falling like drops of blood.



The primal scent raged its raging, shrieking the midnight silence. Piece by piece, strand by strand, drop by drop. Heaven and earth, ceaselessly falling. Shards of space-time, blood-red demon eyes, trailing pale blue-white smoke, flickering ghastly blue flames, hurtling in stillness.

A heaven and earth in a single drop of blood—the Milky Way resplendent. Seven hundred billion billion enormous stars forming the blood-arms, stretching freely. A giant spider, a giant web, seventy-seven hundred billion stars clinging to it, each one glistening like a drop of blood.

Softly, passing through the boundless steam, heaven and earth shattered between the fingers. The spider's shriek could not stir the faintest wind, only trailing a pale red arc, sliding past the last stillness. On the bright mirror surface, paths crisscrossed, extinguishing in blood-red.

XVI


"I'll drink mine straight. Adding Sprite to yours?"

"No!"

"Good wine needs to breathe. Let's talk business first."

"Fine. I know a few people who deal in red wine. Two of the brands are pretty well known. Mr. Wang likes red wine?"

"Ha, it's alright. I usually drink baijiu more. See—drank again at lunch!"

"Are you still okay now?"

"Haha, no problem."

He'd probably had too much at lunch. He kept drifting off, unable to focus on the business at hand.

"Mr. Wang, the wine should be ready now. Let's just leave the business as is—have the people below continue the follow-up!"

"Fine. Let's drink first."

He called a waiter over. Wine was poured, dishes served.
