A Quatrain for the New Year, and Blog on Pause for 7 Days

2008/2/5 15:35:31

Happy New Year

Chán Zhōng Shuō Chán

Firecracker sounds, joyfully bidding the old farewell
Door couplets on every household, renewed at last
The Dipper turns over the Dragon Realm — ten thousand autumns of years
Brilliant sun under Yao's sky — ten thousand li of spring
