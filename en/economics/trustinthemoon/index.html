You May Not Believe in Mooncakes, But You Must Believe in the Moon

2007/9/26 15:56:37

How powerful the moon is — you've all witnessed it these past two days. You may not believe in mooncakes, but you must believe in the moon. Why? Even the earth's tides must plagiarize the moon — is that flimsy stock market more tidal than the tides? Yesterday it was already made clear: "If not for someone propping things up for new stock listings, today's decline would have been far more than this." Yesterday's decline wasn't enough, so today it continued — it's that simple.

This morning's upward push — first, it didn't break through yesterday's third sell point; second, it instead formed the upward thrust of the third K-line in a standard top fractal — regardless of whether you believe in this ID's theory, what followed played out like a textbook. The current trend is extremely simple: a 1-minute trend is currently departing from the preceding 5-minute hub. When this 1-minute trend can complete depends on subsequent price action. And what matters most comes after completion — the pullback. Whether it can form a third sell point — that is the key to near-term price action. If it forms, then these past two days can at most be considered Tian Liang's 1-meter springboard. Whether the performance ahead involves the 3-meter or 10-meter platform, for the audience, it actually doesn't matter anymore.

On individual stocks, today Shuijingfang finally performed as Shuijingfang should. Look at this stock — this is what "churning" means. For almost a year, it just ground away here. Why? Because there are N groups of people inside it, all bullish, so they just churn against each other. Is this time for real or not? Heaven knows. Why? Because whenever someone makes the first move, all N parties start churning again. Actually, this game is quite fun — it just wastes a stock with such excellent fundamentals.

However, good stocks ultimately shine. Take 000999 — from when this ID first discussed it here to now, it's been nearly 10 months. Starting at 6 yuan grabbing shares, then the battle with traitor funds around 12 yuan, then another crowd of bored people piling in at 15 yuan, messing around left and right. In between, rumors flew everywhere. And the result? Look at how much the new major shareholder paid to get in. Someone who paid that much — what big plans they have — is there really any question? This ID has repeatedly said to hold through thick and thin, but when it actually starts happening, many people will probably get confused by market fluctuations.

Every stock is a story — never-ending ones. After completing one stock, you become a person with stories.

A reminder once again: regarding those two stocks mentioned yesterday, if the market has major problems, they'll adjust too. But due to their potentially transformative fundamentals, if the market experiences big volatility, it will provide a good entry opportunity. The stocks this ID mentions are always from a medium-to-long-term perspective — there's plenty of time for you to enter. The key is watching for buy and sell points.

I can answer questions until 4:30 today.
