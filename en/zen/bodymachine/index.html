The Machine Side of the Human Body

2008/5/11 18:51:56

Today's scheduled hydration was suspended due to an episode of unexpected bleeding in the early morning hours. In fact, the cervical lumps have already turned into a granular connected state, and the blood was coming from these connections. Each time this polluted blood comes out, the lumps shrink somewhat. But the doctors don't care much about this phenomenon — they just want to proceed with chemotherapy as fast as possible.

The blood volume was significant. After the doctors treated it, the circulation of qi and blood became chaotic. At noon there was a sensation of suffocation. Then, using three bowls of herbal soup, qi and blood were restored to normal, and immediately afterward a large amount of black material was expelled. The body was restored as if nothing had happened. Then I proposed that since the body had recovered, hydration should begin tomorrow. To some extent, the human body has a machine-like aspect — the key is whether you can grasp its critical switches.

Barring unforeseen circumstances, the entire chemotherapy is delayed by one day. The most dangerous phase will likely be Tuesday morning. Tomorrow is just hydration — just over ten hours of IV fluids.

Record complete.

This ID has seen everyone's messages. For the sake of your sincerity, this ID cannot fall either. The human heart is made of flesh; it knows warmth and cold.

Signing off now, goodbye.
