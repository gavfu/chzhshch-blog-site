My First Intimate Encounter with Cancer

2008/4/24 19:37:32

This happened when I was 17 and had just entered university. At the time, through meditation practice, I had just unlocked the body's energy. Sometimes while sitting in a classroom, as soon as I entered even a slight meditative state, people around me would cough incessantly. Later, they all said that whenever this ID sat in the back, halos of light would appear on the walls. But these were not truly definitive empirical evidence—not until that one time.

That time, I went to a tumor hospital and entered the room of a bone cancer patient. The person was very thin, lying there, nothing but skin and bones. Out of curiosity, this ID placed my right hand on his forehead and my left hand on his abdomen. The man's body suddenly jolted as if being charged with electricity—his feet lifted off the bed, suspended in the air, and he kept expelling air from his mouth. The moment this ID removed my hands, he returned to normal. That incident truly frightened this ID. Afterward, I repeated the experiment several times, and the results were always the same.

This was this ID's first intimate encounter with cancer. From that point on, the karmic entanglement with it probably began.

Later, this ID came to understand that these so-called abilities were not the right path. Then, like shedding skin, I stripped away all these so-called abilities, and afterward became just like an ordinary person—no matter how hard one tried, that energy was gone.

I share these things only to tell the truth about what this ID personally experienced. It will likely be beneficial to those with a discerning mind.
